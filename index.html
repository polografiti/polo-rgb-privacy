Polo RGB — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-29 · App ID com.polocreations.polorgb

Polo RGB does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

The app is a local Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) controller for ELK-BLEDOM-compatible RGB LED strips. All communication happens directly between your phone and your LED controller — no servers, no analytics, no tracking, no third parties.

What the app accesses

PermissionWhy it's neededWhere it goes
BLUETOOTH_SCAN
(Android 12+)		 Discover nearby ELK-BLEDOM controllers Local, never leaves the device
BLUETOOTH_CONNECT
(Android 12+)		 Pair and exchange commands with the LED controller Local, never leaves the device
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
(Android 6–11)		 Required by older Android versions for BLE scanning. The app declares neverForLocation on Android 12+ so location is not used. Local, never leaves the device

The neverForLocation flag explicitly tells Android that the app does not use Bluetooth scan results to derive your location.

What the app stores

The following information is stored only on your device in the app's private storage:

  • The names you assign to your saved LED controllers
  • The Bluetooth MAC addresses of those controllers
  • Your saved color and effect presets (color, effect, brightness, speed)

This data is never transmitted off your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

What the app does NOT do

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no login
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting
  • No advertising or ad SDKs
  • No data sold or shared with third parties
  • No internet connection required (the app never connects to any server)
  • No location tracking

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds an optional cloud feature), the change will be reflected in this page with a new "Last updated" date. The current source-of-truth copy lives in the project repository's PRIVACY_POLICY.md file.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please open an issue on the project's GitHub repository or contact Polo Creations directly.
